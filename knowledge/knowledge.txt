Q: How much will the Japan Trip cost?

Q: Can I join late or leave early during the Japan Trip?
Keywords: join late, leave early, midway, partial participation, late arrival, early departure, schedule, Japan Trip
Answer: Yes, that's fine. However, the participation fee is the same regardless of how long you join, so we recommend attending the full Japan Trip.
Q: Can I skip the Japan Trip and join just a few events?

Keywords: book flight, flight booking, waitlist, application confirmation, flight timing, Japan Trip flights
Answer: Your spot is only confirmed once applications open at the Info Session and you've paid the deposit. We recommend booking your flight after that. If you're comfortable with the risk of ending up on the waitlist, you're welcome to book earlier.
Q: When do finals end, and when does the spring quarter start?
Keywords: finals, final exams, spring quarter, academic calendar, FTMBA schedule, UCLA Anderson finals, Japan Trip timing
Answer: Core final exams run through March 18, and spring quarter instruction begins on March 29.
Q: Are there any recommended flights or arrival/departure times for the Japan Trip?
Keywords: recommended flights, arrival time, departure time, LAX flights, flight schedule, arrival date, Japan Trip flights
Answer: Since finals run through Thursday, March 18, we recommend leaving LAX on March 18 or 19 and arriving in Kyoto on Saturday, March 20. For the return trip, you can fly out of Japan on Saturday, March 27. Depending on your class schedule, you may be able to stay longer. Be careful with flights leaving LA on the afternoon of March 19, as the time difference could make your arrival too late.
Q: What is the best way to get from Los Angeles (LA) to Kyoto for the Japan Trip?
Keywords: LAX to Kyoto, travel route, KIX, Kansai Airport, Narita, Haneda, Itami, ITM, Shinkansen, ZIPAIR, transit, Japan Trip
Answer: Kansai International Airport (KIX) is the closest major international airport to Kyoto. From KIX, you can take a train or bus to Kyoto. If you can't find a suitable flight to KIX, you can fly into Tokyo (Haneda/HND or Narita/NRT) and take a domestic flight to Osaka's Itami Airport (ITM), or travel to Shinagawa and take the Shinkansen (bullet train) to Kyoto. ZIPAIR, a low-cost airline owned by Japan Airlines, may be the cheapest option, but it only flies between LAX and Narita.
Q: What time does the Japan Trip start on March 21?
Keywords: March 21, Day 1 start time, arrival time, schedule, Japan Trip start
Answer: There are no scheduled events that day. You'll just need to arrive in Japan and check into the hotel, and the rest of the day is yours.
Q: Can I join the Japan Trip on the morning of March 22?
Keywords: March 22 arrival, join morning, chartered bus, bus departure time, Japan Trip start
Answer: Our chartered bus will leave the hotel at 9:30 AM, so we recommend checking in the day before.
Q: What time does the Japan Trip end on March 27?
Keywords: March 27, Day 8 end time, departure time, check-out, Japan Trip end
Answer: There are no scheduled activities that day. You'll just check out of the hotel, so you can leave whenever works for you.

Answer: You can bring one significant other. The participation fee will be the same as for Anderson students.

Keywords: onsen, hot springs, tattoos, tattoo policy, bath rules, Hiroshima onsen, Japan Trip onsen
Answer: Guests with tattoos are officially not allowed to use public baths in Japan. In practice, though, we do not expect this to be an issue as long as you avoid disturbing other guests, such as by being noisy. If you are concerned, it is best to cover your tattoos with waterproof stickers or similar coverings. Note that we plan to reserve the hotel onsen in Hiroshima exclusively for our group, so tattoos will not be an issue there.
